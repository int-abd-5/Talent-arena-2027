# ADR-NNNN: the decision, stated as a decision rather than as a topic

- **Status**: proposed | accepted | superseded
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders**: who
- **Supersedes**: ADR-NNNN, or "none"

## Context

What forces this decision, in terms of requirements rather than preferences. Cite the requirement, the
invariant or the measured number that makes this a decision rather than a taste.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated plainly enough that a reader can tell whether a given piece of code conforms.

## Alternatives considered

Each rejected option, with the reason it was rejected. Order by how likely someone is to propose it
again. If an option was rejected for a reason that could change, say so: that reason usually becomes the
revisit trigger.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, and what it makes hard. Name the cost explicitly. A record with no stated cost has
not finished thinking.

## Revisit trigger

The condition under which this decision should be reopened. A fact about the world (a measured
threshold, a customer requirement, a spike result, a licence outcome), not a date. If no such condition
can be stated, the decision is probably an assumption and should say so.
