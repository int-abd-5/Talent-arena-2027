# Product context

Mohib's product truth for MTAH 2027 Possession: what the product is, who it is for, what it must
never do, and the questions still open. **Agents do not rewrite these documents.** Engineering
implements what is written here and does not redefine it.

When engineering analysis forces a product decision, write it as a finding in
`app/technical context/reviews/` addressed to the owner, and wait for the decision. Do not resolve it
silently, and do not answer an open question by invention.

Put the pack here as numbered markdown files (`00_EXECUTIVE_CONTEXT.md`, …). Imported documents keep
their original wording; the owner's agreed edits are scoped and changelogged.
