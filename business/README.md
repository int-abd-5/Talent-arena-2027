# STAR STRUCK Phase 1 brief

**As of** 17 November 2026. **Owner** Mohib.

1. **Problem (sourced).** Estimate the team with controlled possession and identify when the ball is dead.
2. **Reader (confirmed).** The submission is for the registered STAR STRUCK team and contest organizers.
3. **Deliverable (sourced).** Supply 199,319 tactical video frames as a CSV plus public code and a technical report.
4. **Selection (sourced).** The top 20 advance. The official scorer ranks possession spell F1 and breaks ties with frame macro F1.
5. **Price (assumed).** The qualifier has no commercial product scope.
6. **Data (sourced).** Only algorithmic labels are permitted on the competition match. Keep participant data local and assess choices on permitted external games.
7. **Workspace (confirmed).** The official repository is a separate terminal clone. This local repository holds the team's implementation.
8. **Hard part (sourced).** The ball is missing in roughly 41% of graded frames, and loose balls can pass near the opposing team without changing possession.
9. **Delivery (confirmed).** Build a reproducible estimator and write the rationale and validation evidence. Publication requires separate authorization.
10. **OPEN.** Exact cutoff hour and time zone from the participant email. The published deadline is 17 November 2026.
