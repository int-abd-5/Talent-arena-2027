# MTAH 2027 Possession — the business on one page

**What this folder is.** The high-level, human-readable truth about what we are building, for whom,
for how much, under which rules, and what we do now. Read it before anything else. It is short on
purpose; each numbered file links to the detailed evidence.

**As of:** 2026-11-17. **Owner:** Mohib. Update this folder when a decision changes. Do not let
it drift into another specification.

## The business in ten lines

Fill each line with one or two sentences, and mark each claim's evidence class (see below). Leave a
line as `OPEN: <the question>` rather than inventing an answer.

1. **Problem.** OPEN: who loses what today, and how much?
2. **Customer.** OPEN: who buys, and who uses it?
3. **Product.** Estimate team possession and stoppages from tactical tracking for the More Than A Hack 2027 qualifier.
4. **Why us / why now.** OPEN
5. **Price.** OPEN
6. **Constraints and compliance.** OPEN: contracts, regulation, the never-do list.
7. **What exists.** Nothing yet beyond this scaffold.
8. **The hard part.** OPEN: usually reaching customers and getting their data, not the code.
9. **What we do next.** B0: the reproducible workspace and verification pipeline (see BUILD_PROGRESS).
10. **When we stop.** OPEN: the evidence that would end this project.

## Evidence classes

Every number or claim in this folder is one of four kinds, and says which:

- **confirmed** by a real customer;
- **sourced**, with the source named;
- **simulated** (a panel, a model, a guess dressed as data);
- **assumed**.

Most early numbers are simulated or assumed. Say so plainly, and never publish an unverified rating,
pass rate, licence number or review for a real business.
