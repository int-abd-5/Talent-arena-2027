# Research

Evidence for the claims in [the business README](../README.md): interviews, market notes, competitor
reads, panels. Keep raw material here, immutable once filed; put corrections on the page above it.

Keep an `EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md` here that lists each claim, its evidence class (confirmed, sourced,
simulated, assumed) and where the evidence lives, so a reviewer can search the register instead of
reading transcripts whole.
